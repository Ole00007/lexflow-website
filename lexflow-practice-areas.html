In depth

How LexFlow fits each practice area

Each area below shows what it covers, an example intake, and how a matter moves through a firm's stages. These are descriptions of how a firm might organise its work, not guidance on any individual case.

Definition

Civil law covers disputes between private parties about obligations, contracts, property and compensation, rather than offences prosecuted by the state.

Intake example

A homeowner asks the firm to review a renovation contract after the builder stopped work and kept the deposit.

Workflow example

The intake is captured, a conflict check and a limitation-date check are done, the matter is assigned to a civil lawyer, the client's matter page shows the current stage and the next step, and the firm records each approved update as the dispute progresses.

See how LexFlow stages a civil matter

Definition

Corporate and business law covers how companies are formed, governed, bought and sold, and how their contracts, boards and compliance obligations are documented.

Intake example

Two founders ask the firm to prepare a shareholders' agreement and a first set of standard contracts before they sign with their first client.

Workflow example

The company record is created once, the matter is routed to a corporate lawyer, entity documents and contract templates are attached to the company file, approval steps are logged, and each signed document is filed back against the same company record.

Explore the corporate workspace setup

Definition

Family law covers matters between people who are or were in a family relationship, including separation, arrangements for children, and the division of shared property.

Intake example

A parent contacts the firm after a separation to ask about arranging contact with their children and dividing a jointly owned home.

Workflow example

The intake is recorded with the parties involved, a conflict check is run before anything else, the matter is opened with restricted access so only the assigned team can view the file, sensitive documents are stored against the matter, and hearing dates are tracked with reminders.

Read how LexFlow handles sensitive family files

Definition

Criminal defence work covers representing someone who is under investigation or charged with an offence, from the first contact through the hearing and any appeal.

Intake example

A person calls the firm after receiving a notice to appear at a hearing and asks for a lawyer to attend.

Workflow example

The urgent intake is flagged, a court-date entry is created with reminders, the assigned lawyer records attendance notes against the matter, evidence items are listed with their status, and every deadline is surfaced on a single timeline.

See how LexFlow tracks court dates

Definition

Real estate law covers transactions and disputes involving land and buildings, such as sales, leases, title questions and the steps required to complete a transfer.

Intake example

A buyer asks the firm to handle the purchase of an apartment and to check the title before completion.

Workflow example

The matter is opened from a property template, a checklist of required documents is generated, each item is marked as received or outstanding, key dates such as completion are tracked, and the client page shows which steps are done and which are next.

See the real estate checklist workflow

Definition

Intellectual property law covers rights in creations and brands, including trade marks, patents and copyright, and the steps needed to register, licence and enforce them.

Intake example

A small studio asks the firm to register a trade mark for a new product name and to review a licence agreement with a distributor.

Workflow example

Each right is recorded in a portfolio with its owner, status and renewal date, filing deadlines trigger reminders, licence documents are attached to the same record, and the next action for each right is shown on one board.

See how LexFlow manages an IP portfolio

Definition

Immigration law covers applications and appeals about a person's right to enter, stay or work in a country, including family, study and protection routes.

Intake example

A family asks the firm to prepare a residence application and to keep track of the documents each member must provide.

Workflow example

A document checklist is created per applicant, each item is tracked until received, application and appointment dates are logged with reminders, translations and authenticated document copies are filed against the matter, and the client sees a plain status for each application.

See how LexFlow tracks multi-applicant matters

Definition

Labour and employment law covers the relationship between employers and workers, including contracts, working conditions, disciplinary processes and disputes.

Intake example

An employer asks the firm to review a new employment contract template and to set up a process for handling internal grievances.

Workflow example

The template is versioned in one place, the matter is routed to an employment lawyer, deadlines for response are tracked, each grievance is recorded as a separate matter linked to the company, and the client sees the current stage of every open item.

Explore the employment intake template

Definition

Administrative law covers the relationship between people and public bodies, including permits, licences, regulatory decisions and appeals against those decisions.

Intake example

A business asks the firm to challenge a refused permit and to track the deadline for lodging an appeal.

Workflow example

The decision letter is attached to the matter, the appeal deadline is calculated and tracked, correspondence with the public body is logged, each filing is recorded with its submission date, and the client page shows the next step and its date.

See how LexFlow tracks regulatory deadlines

Definition

Constitutional law deals with the rules that govern how the state is organised and how fundamental rights are protected, including review of laws and public acts.

Intake example

A rights organisation asks the firm to prepare a submission arguing that a measure affects a protected right.

Workflow example

The matter is opened with the legal questions recorded, authorities and sources are filed in a shared research space, drafts pass through review stages with tracked changes, and the submission and response dates are visible on the matter timeline.

See how LexFlow organises research and review

Definition

Human rights work covers claims and advocacy about the protection of people's basic rights, including asylum and international protection matters.

Intake example

A support group asks the firm to document a series of incidents and to prepare the paperwork for a protection claim.

Workflow example

Statements and supporting documents are collected against a single file, each incident is logged with a date and a reference, access is limited to the assigned team, and the chronology is built automatically for the lawyer to review.

See how LexFlow keeps a secure chronology

Definition

Crimes against the person covers offences that affect a person's safety or integrity, such as threats, assault and serious bodily harm, and the proceedings that follow.

Intake example

A person asks the firm for support after reporting an assault and wants the firm to follow the proceedings.

Workflow example

The intake is recorded with the key dates, a conflict check is completed, the matter is assigned, hearing dates and deadlines are tracked with reminders, statements and reports are filed in order, and the client sees the current stage without internal notes.

See how LexFlow separates the client view from internal notes
