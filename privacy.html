LexFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. LexFlow handles client data under EU GDPR, ISO 27001:2022, and ISO/IEC 27701:2025.

1. Who we are

LexFlow is a legal practice management platform operated for Studio Legale X (Genoa, Italy) and deployed by AVibe Agency. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is protected.

2. Data we collect

  • Intake form data: name, email, phone, practice area, and message when you request a demo.
  • Chatbot messages: messages sent to the Alessia assistant, used to route your request to the right team member.
  • Usage data (anonymous): pages visited and language/theme preferences, stored locally in your browser (localStorage).

3. How we use data

  • To respond to demo requests and route them to the firm's CRM.
  • To send notifications about your request (to the firm and to the interested client).
  • To improve the website experience.

4. Data protection

All data is transmitted over TLS. Client matter data in the LexFlow CRM is stored with bank-grade 256-bit encryption, zero-knowledge document storage, and SOC2-aligned practices. Internal notes are never visible to clients.

5. Cookies & local storage

LexFlow does not use advertising trackers. We use localStorage only for your language and theme preferences. A cookie consent banner is shown on first visit; you may dismiss it at any time.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to info@lexflow.com. We respond within 30 days.
