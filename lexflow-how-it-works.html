How It Works

From intake to resolution, in three calm steps

LexFlow removes the back-and-forth between firm and client. No client login, no installs. Just a secure link and a clear timeline.

The Client Journey
The Client Journey

Three steps, zero friction

1

Client submits

The client fills out a short intake form from any device — phone, tablet, or desktop. No account, no download.

2

Lawyer reviews & acts

The matter lands directly in the firm's role-based dashboard. The assigned lawyer or paralegal reviews, adds internal notes, and updates status.

3

Client tracks live

The client opens their unique, token-secured link at any time to see real-time status — no phone calls needed to ask "where are we?"
